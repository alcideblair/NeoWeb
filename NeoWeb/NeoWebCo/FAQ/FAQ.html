Frequently Asked Questions

Learn how Neo Web builds smart website experiences starting at 9,999 PHP.

What services does Neo Web offer?

Neo Web specializes in website design, responsive web development, SEO optimization, and launch support for small businesses, startups, and growing brands.

How much does a website cost?

Our website packages start at 9,999 PHP. This base price is ideal for a clean, modern landing page or small business website with essential pages and mobile responsiveness.

What is included in the 9,999 PHP starting package?

The starter package includes design consultation, responsive layout, up to 5 sections/pages, basic SEO setup, and a simple contact form. Additional features are quoted separately.

How long does a website project take?

Most websites are delivered within 1 to 4 weeks, depending on scope, content readiness, and revision requests. We keep timelines clear so you know when your site will launch.

Can I update the website myself?

Yes. We can build your site with an easy content management system or provide a handoff guide so you can update text, images, and pricing without a developer.

Do you offer hosting, domain setup, or maintenance?

Yes, we can help with hosting recommendations, domain registration, email setup, and ongoing maintenance packages to keep your website secure and up to date.

What do you need to get started?

We typically request your business name, branding style, target audience, content or copy, and any examples of websites you like. The more detail you provide, the faster we can begin.

How can I request a quote?

Contact us through the main site form, email contact@neoweb.com, or call +63 905 334 9524. We’ll schedule a short consultation and send a personalized proposal.
